## Safe headline template

Before the live matrix is complete:

> FirstGreen is an HPC-inspired runtime that extracts a bounded task DAG from a software goal and
> schedules isolated coding agents along its critical path, with verified delivery as the measured
> completion boundary.

After the matrix, append exactly one scoped sentence:

> On the frozen Ramsey workload using `MODEL`, median time to verified delivery changed from `T1`
> at one slot to `TN` at `N` slots (`SPEEDUP×`); results are workload-, model-, and host-specific.

Never replace that sentence with “N agents are N× faster” or a general claim about coding agents.
